## Proverbs 1:1 (id: 10020001001)

1Long buk ya i gat ol waestok blong King Solomon, king blong Isrel,\* we papa blong hem King Deved.

## Proverbs 1:2 (id: 10020001002)

2Ol tok ya i blong givhan long yumi blong mekem yumi waes, blong yumi save folem ol gudfala advaes, mo yumi save haremsave ol tok we mining blong olgeta i dip.

## Proverbs 1:3 (id: 10020001003)

3Ol tok ya i save tijim yumi long fasin we bambae yumi save gat hed, mo we yumi no save giaman, mo we yumi save mekem ol fasin we i stret, mo we yumi save mekem i sem mak nomo long olgeta man.

## Proverbs 1:4 (id: 10020001004)

4Ol tok ya i save openem tingting blong man we i no save samting yet, mo i save mekem ol yang man oli waes gud, mo oli gat gudfala tingting.

## Proverbs 1:5 (id: 10020001005)

5Ol tok ya i save mekem ol waes man oli gat save moa, mo i save lidim ol man we oli skul gud,

## Proverbs 1:6 (id: 10020001006)

6blong oli save luksave mining blong ol waestok we i haed gud, mo oli save givim ansa long ol had kwestin we ol waes man oli stap askem.

## Proverbs 1:7 (id: 10020001007)

7Sipos man i wantem kasem save, stamba blong hem, hem i mas tinghevi long Hae God, mo i mas ona gud long hem. Man we i no gat hed, hem i no save tinghevi long ol waestok, mo hem i no wantem lan.

## Proverbs 1:8 (id: 10020001008)

8Pikinini blong mi, yu yu mas lesin gud long ol samting we mi wetem mama blong yu, mitufala i stap tijim yu long hem, mo yu no mas lego ol samting ya.

## Proverbs 1:9 (id: 10020001009)

9Ol tok ya bambae i givhan, blong mekem laef blong yu i gud moa. Bambae i olsem gudfala hat blong flasem hed blong yu, no gudfala jen blong flasem nek blong yu.

## Proverbs 1:10 (id: 10020001010)

10Pikinini blong mi, sipos ol man nogud oli kam traem pulum yu, blong yu go wetem olgeta, be yu no mas go.

## Proverbs 1:11 (id: 10020001011)

11Maet oli save talem long yu se, “!Yu kam! Bambae yumi go haed long saed blong rod, mo sipos yumi luk sam man, nating we oli no mekem sam samting nogud, be bambae yumi mekem faet long olgeta, yumi kilim olgeta i ded. Bambae yumi mekem blong pleplei nomo.

## Proverbs 1:12 (id: 10020001012)

12Nating sipos oli stap gud nomo, be taem faet i finis, bambae i no moa gat man i stap.

## Proverbs 1:13 (id: 10020001013)

13Mo sipos oli gat ol gudgudfala samting, bambae yumi karem, yumi kam fulumap olgeta long ol haos blong yumi.

## Proverbs 1:14 (id: 10020001014)

14!Yu kam joen wetem mifala! !Yumi go! Sipos yumi kasem sam samting, bambae yumi seraot long yumi evriwan.”

## Proverbs 1:15 (id: 10020001015)

15Pikinini blong mi, yu no mas go wetem ol man olsem. Yu mas stap longwe long olgeta.

## Proverbs 1:16 (id: 10020001016)

16Fasin blong ol man olsem, oltaem oli kwik blong mekem ol samting nogud, oli no save wet. Oli rere oltaem nomo blong kilim man i ded.

## Proverbs 1:17 (id: 10020001017)

17I krangke blong man i go hangem net blong kasem pijin, we pijin ya i stap lukluk hem,

## Proverbs 1:18 (id: 10020001018)

18be ol man olsem oli stap wokem trap blong olgeta nomo, mo bambae oli ded long trap ya bakegen.

## Proverbs 1:19 (id: 10020001019)

19!Fasin ya we man i stap go kilim man blong i stil long hem, hem i rod blong ded nomo! Oltaem nomo, man we i stap mekem olsem, hem i mas ded.

## Proverbs 1:20 (id: 10020001020)

20!Yufala i lesin! Naoia waestok i olsem man we i stap singaot olbaot long ol big rod, mo long ol ples blong mekem maket.

## Proverbs 1:21 (id: 10020001021)

21Hem i stap singaot bigwan long ol doa\* blong stonwol blong taon, mo long ol ples we plante man oli stap hivap long hem.

## Proverbs 1:22 (id: 10020001022)

22Hem i talem se, “!Olgeta! !Yufala we i no gat hed! ?Bambae yufala i stap krangke olsem gogo kasem wetaem? ?Naoia yufala i glad tumas blong jikim gudfala save, be bambae yufala i finis wetaem? ?From wanem yufala i no save lan?

## Proverbs 1:23 (id: 10020001023)

23Naoia, bambae mi mi tok long yufala blong stretem yufala, mo yufala i mas lesin gud long mi. Bambae mi givim gudfala tok long yufala blong givhan long yufala, mo bambae mi mekem tok blong mi i klia long yufala.

## Proverbs 1:24 (id: 10020001024)

24Longtaem finis, mi mi stap singaot yufala, be yufala i no wantem harem mi. Mi stap traem pulum yufala blong yufala i kam long mi, be yufala i no wantem.

## Proverbs 1:25 (id: 10020001025)

25Yufala i no wantem lesin long tok blong mi, we mi givim blong givhan long yufala, mo yufala i no glad blong letem mi mi stretem yufala.

## Proverbs 1:26 (id: 10020001026)

26Be sipos trabol i kasem yufala, mi bambae mi laf nomo long yufala. Sipos yufala i fraet we i fraet,

## Proverbs 1:27 (id: 10020001027)

27we tingting blong yufala i raon, olsem we bigfala waelwin i tantanem yufala, mo yufala i stap harem nogud tumas long bodi blong yufala, be mi bambae mi stap jikim yufala nomo.

## Proverbs 1:28 (id: 10020001028)

28Sipos i olsem, bambae yufala i singaot mi, be bambae mi no save lesin long yufala. Bambae yufala i lukaot mi long evri ples, be bambae yufala i no save faenem mi.

## Proverbs 1:29 (id: 10020001029)

29Yufala ya, neva yufala i wantem blong yufala i gat save, mo neva yufala i wantem obei long Hae God.

## Proverbs 1:30 (id: 10020001030)

30Neva yufala i wantem blong mi mi givim tok long yufala blong givhan long yufala, mo taem we mi mi wantem stretem yufala, be neva yufala i lesin long mi.

## Proverbs 1:31 (id: 10020001031)

31Nao from samting ya, bambae yufala i kasem panis we i stret blong yufala i kasem, mo ol fasin ya we yufala i stap mekem, bambae oli mekem we yufala i sik from.

## Proverbs 1:32 (id: 10020001032)

32Ol man we oli no gat hed, bambae oli ded from we oli no wantem kasem waes. Mo ol man we oli no gat save, mo we oli no kea, fasin ya blong olgeta bambae i kilim olgeta i ded.

## Proverbs 1:33 (id: 10020001033)

33Be man we i stap lesin long mi, bambae hem i stap gud oltaem long laef blong hem. Hem bambae i save we i no gat wan samting nating we i save mekem hem i fraet.”

## Proverbs 2:1 (id: 10020002001)

1Pikinini blong mi. Yu mas lanem ol samting ya we mi stap tijim yu long hem, mo yu no mas fogetem ol fasin ya we mi stap talem long yu se yu mas mekem.

## Proverbs 2:2 (id: 10020002002)

2Yu mas lesin long ol waestok ya, mo yu mas traehad blong haremsave olgeta.

## Proverbs 2:3 (id: 10020002003)

3Yes, yu mas askem strong long Hae God, blong i mekem yu yu gat save, mo yu mas askem long hem oltaem, blong yu save haremsave mining blong ol strong samting.

## Proverbs 2:4 (id: 10020002004)

4Oltaem yu mas traehad blong kasem waes ya, olsem we yu save traehad blong faenem silva, no wan narafala samting olsem, we man i bin berem.

## Proverbs 2:5 (id: 10020002005)

5Sipos yu stap mekem olsem, bambae yu save kasem mining blong tok ya we i talem se, “Yu mas tinghevi long Hae God, mo yu mas ona gud long hem.” Nao bambae yu save fasin blong hem moa, blong yu savegud hem.

## Proverbs 2:6 (id: 10020002006)

6Hae God nomo i save mekem man i waes. Fasin blong save mo fasin blong haremsave, tufala i stap kamaot long hem nomo.

## Proverbs 2:7 (id: 10020002007)

7Hem i stap givhan long ol stret man we oli no save giaman, mo i stap blokemgud olgeta blong trabol i no kasem olgeta.

## Proverbs 2:8 (id: 10020002008)

8Hem i stap lukaot gud long olgeta we oli stap mekem i stret nomo long ol narafala man, mo i stap gad gud long olgeta we tingting blong olgeta i stap strong long hem.

## Proverbs 2:9 (id: 10020002009)

9Nao sipos yu harem tok blong mi, bambae yu save luksave ol fasin we i gud, mo yu save jajem ol man long stret fasin, mo yu save mekem i stret long olgeta man, mo bambae yu save olgeta samting we i stret blong yu mekem.

## Proverbs 2:10 (id: 10020002010)

10Nao bambae yu save kam waes, mo waes ya blong yu bambae i save mekem yu yu harem gud.

## Proverbs 2:11 (id: 10020002011)

11Fasin ya we yu luksave ol samting, mo we yu savegud ol mining blong hem, hem bambae i stap lukaot long yu,

## Proverbs 2:12 (id: 10020002012)

12mo bambae i stap blokemgud yu, blong yu no mekem ol fasin nogud. Mo bambae i mekem we yu stap longwe long ol man we toktok blong olgeta i blong statem trabol nomo.

## Proverbs 2:13 (id: 10020002013)

13Ol man olsem oli lego stret laef finis, oli stap long fasin blong tudak nomo.

## Proverbs 2:14 (id: 10020002014)

14Olgeta oli glad blong mekem ol samting nogud, mo oli stap harem gud blong mekem ol rabis fasin ya.

## Proverbs 2:15 (id: 10020002015)

15Ol man olsem, yu no save putum tingting blong yu i stap long olgeta, mo yu no save trastem olgeta nating.

## Proverbs 2:16 (id: 10020002016)

16Sipos yu harem tok blong mi, bambae yu save blokem woman nogud, we i traem blong switim yu long ol swit tok blong hem.

## Proverbs 2:17 (id: 10020002017)

17Woman olsem i gowe long man blong hem, we i mared long hem long taem ya we i yangfala yet, mo i fogetem promes blong hem we i mekem long fes blong Hae God.

## Proverbs 2:18 (id: 10020002018)

18Sipos yu go long haos blong woman olsem, hemia i min se yu stap wokbaot long rod blong ded nomo, mo yu stap go klosap long Hom\* blong ol Dedman.

## Proverbs 2:19 (id: 10020002019)

19Man we oltaem i stap go insaed long haos blong woman olsem, bambae hem i no save lego fasin ya, mo neva bambae hem i save kambak bakegen long rod blong laef.

## Proverbs 2:20 (id: 10020002020)

20From samting ya, yu mas folem fasin blong gudfala man. Oltaem yu mas wokbaot long rod we i stret.

## Proverbs 2:21 (id: 10020002021)

21Ol stret man we yu save trastem olgeta, bambae oli save stap gud long ples ya blong yumi.

## Proverbs 2:22 (id: 10020002022)

22Be ol man nogud we oli stap mekem ol rabis fasin, bambae God i tekemaot olgeta oli gowe long ples ya. Hem bambae i rutumaot olgeta, olsem we man i stap rutumaot ol smosmol tri long garen.

## Proverbs 3:1 (id: 10020003001)

1Pikinini blong mi. Yu no mas fogetem ol samting ya we mi mi stap tijim yu long hem. Oltaem yu mas tingbaot ol tok blong mi.

## Proverbs 3:2 (id: 10020003002)

2Sipos yu mekem olsem, bambae yu no save sot long wan samting long laef blong yu, mo bambae yu gat longfala laef.

## Proverbs 3:3 (id: 10020003003)

3Bambae yu no mas lego promes we yu mekem wetem God, we tingting blong yu i stap strong long hem, mo yu no mas lego promes we yu mekem wetem ol fren blong yu, we tingting blong yu i stap strong long olgeta. Oltaem, yu mas holemtaet tufala fasin ya, olsem we yu raetemdaon long tingting blong yu, no yu hangem long nek blong yu, blong tufala i no lus.

## Proverbs 3:4 (id: 10020003004)

4Sipos yu stap mekem olsem, bambae God i glad long yu, mo ol man tu bambae oli glad long yu.

## Proverbs 3:5 (id: 10020003005)

5I nogud yu ting se save ya blong yu naoia, hem i naf blong givhan long yu. Yu mas trastem Hae God long olgeta tingting blong yu.

## Proverbs 3:6 (id: 10020003006)

6Long olgeta samting we yu stap mekem, yu mas tingbaot hem oltaem, mo sipos yu stap mekem olsem, hem bambae i soemaot stret rod long yu, blong yu yu folem.

## Proverbs 3:7 (id: 10020003007)

7I nogud yu ting se yu yu wan waes man. Yu mas tinghevi long Hae God, yu mas ona gud long hem, mo yu mas tanem baksaed long ol fasin nogud.

## Proverbs 3:8 (id: 10020003008)

8Sipos yu mekem olsem, fasin ya bambae i olsem gudfala meresin we i save mekem ol soa blong yu i finis, mo i save mekem ol sobodi blong yu i no moa soa.

## Proverbs 3:9 (id: 10020003009)

9Yu mas tekem ol fas kakae blong garen blong yu, wetem ol nambawan samting long ol narafala kakae we yu kasem long hem, yu givim i go long Hae God, blong yu ona long hem.

## Proverbs 3:10 (id: 10020003010)

10Sipos yu mekem olsem, haos blong putumgud kakae blong yu long hem, oltaem bambae i fulap long kakae, mo bambae yu kasem fulap waen, we ol botel blong yu oli no naf blong holem.

## Proverbs 3:11 (id: 10020003011)

11Pikinini blong mi. Sipos Hae God i mekem wan samting blong stretem yu, be yu mas glad blong karem, mo yu mas save we hem i mekem samting ya blong wekemap yu.

## Proverbs 3:12 (id: 10020003012)

12Ol man we hem i lavem olgeta, oltaem hem i stap stretem olgeta, olsem we papa i stap stretem pikinini blong hem, we i glad tumas long hem.

## Proverbs 3:13 (id: 10020003013)

13Man we i stap kasem save, mo we i stap kam waes, hem bambae i save harem gud,

## Proverbs 3:14 (id: 10020003014)

14from we fasin ya i gud moa, i winim we hem i kasem silva, no gol.

## Proverbs 3:15 (id: 10020003015)

15Waestok i gud moa, i winim ol gudfala ston ya we praes blong olgeta i antap tumas, we ol man oli stap yusum blong flasem ring mo jen long hem, mo i no gat wan samting nating we yu save wantem samtaem, we i gud moa winim hem.

## Proverbs 3:16 (id: 10020003016)

16Hem i save givim longfala laef long yu, mo i save givim plante gudgudfala samting tu long yu, mo i save mekem ol man oli ona long yu.

## Proverbs 3:17 (id: 10020003017)

17Hem i save mekem laef blong yu i ron gud, nao bambae yu stap gud oltaem long laef blong yu.

## Proverbs 3:18 (id: 10020003018)

18Ol man we oli holem waestok oli glad oltaem nomo, mo hem bambae i mekem olgeta oli gat laef.

## Proverbs 3:19 (id: 10020003019)

19Hae God i mekem wol ya long waes blong hem, mo long save blong hem, hem i putum skae long prapa ples blong hem.

## Proverbs 3:20 (id: 10020003020)

20Long waes ya blong hem, hem i mekem we ol reva i ron, mo i mekem we ol klaod i givim ren i kam long wol ya.

## Proverbs 3:21 (id: 10020003021)

21Pikinini blong mi. I gud yu holemstrong waes ya blong yu, mo fasin ya blong yu we yu save luksave mining blong ol samting we i had. Yu mas lukaot gud blong yu no lusum tufala samting ya.

## Proverbs 3:22 (id: 10020003022)

22Bambae tufala fasin ya i givim laef long yu, mo laef ya blong yu bambae i gudwan, we bambae yu glad long hem oltaem.

## Proverbs 3:23 (id: 10020003023)

23Nao bambae yu save wokbaot long rod blong yu, we oltaem bambae yu stap gud nomo, yu no save foldaon.

## Proverbs 3:24 (id: 10020003024)

24Mo long naet, taem yu go long bed blong yu, bambae yu save slip gud, we yu no save fraet.

## Proverbs 3:25 (id: 10020003025)

25Bambae trabol i save kam kwiktaem nomo long ol man nogud, olsem we strong win i save girap wantaem nomo i kam, be yu bambae yu no save trabol long samting ya.

## Proverbs 3:26 (id: 10020003026)

26Hae God bambae i holem yu, blong yu save stap gud oltaem, mo bambae hem i no save letem yu yu foldaon long wan trap.

## Proverbs 3:27 (id: 10020003027)

27Sipos yu gat janis blong givhan long narafala man, i gud yu gohed, yu mekem i gud long hem, stret olsem we hem i wantem.

## Proverbs 3:28 (id: 10020003028)

28Sipos wan long olgeta we oli stap raonabaot long yu, i wantem blong yu givhan long hem, mo yu save mekem long taem ya nomo, i nogud yu talem long hem se, “Yu wet, tumora bambae mi kam givhan long yu.”

## Proverbs 3:29 (id: 10020003029)

## Proverbs 3:30 (id: 10020003030)

30Sipos man i no mekem wan samting nogud long yu, i nogud yu rao long hem.

## Proverbs 3:31 (id: 10020003031)

31Mo i nogud yu jalus long ol man blong faet we oli stap win, mo i nogud tu blong yu folem fasin blong olgeta,

## Proverbs 3:32 (id: 10020003032)

32from we Hae God i no laekem nating ol man we oli stap mekem ol samting nogud, i agens long olgeta. Hem i trastem ol stret man, mo i stap talemaot tingting blong hem long olgeta.

33Oltaem, hem i stap singaot\* trabol i kam long haos blong man nogud, be hem i stap blesem haos blong man we laef blong hem i stret.

## Proverbs 3:35 (id: 10020003035)

35Ol waes man bambae oli gat gud nem, mo bambae ol man oli stap ona gud long olgeta, be ol man we oli no gat hed, bambae oli stap mekem ol fasin nomo, we bambae oli sem from.

## Proverbs 4:1 (id: 10020004001)

1Pikinini blong mi. Mi mi papa blong yu, mo yu mas lesin gud long ol samting ya we mi stap tijim yu long hem. Sipos yu lesin gud olsem, bambae yu save gat waes.

## Proverbs 4:2 (id: 10020004002)

2Ol samting ya we mi stap talem long yu, oli gud tumas, mo yu no mas tanem baksaed long olgeta.

## Proverbs 4:3 (id: 10020004003)

3Mi mi wan pikinini nomo we papa mo mama blong mi, tufala i bonem, mo taem mi mi smol,

## Proverbs 4:4 (id: 10020004004)

4papa blong mi i stap tijim mi. Hem i talem long mi se, “Pikinini blong mi, ol samting ya we mi stap talem long yu, bambae yu no mas fogetem olgeta samtaem, oltaem yu mas tingbaot olgeta. Sipos yu stap mekem ol samting ya oltaem, bambae yu gat laef.

## Proverbs 4:5 (id: 10020004005)

5Yu mas kasem waestok, mo yu mas kam we yu save luksave mining blong ol had samting. Yu no mas blokem sora blong yu, mo yu no mas fogetem ol samting ya, we mi stap talem long yu.

## Proverbs 4:6 (id: 10020004006)

6Sipos yu holem waestok ya, bambae hem i stap lukaot gud long yu. Sipos yu laekem hem, hem bambae i holem yu yu stap gud oltaem.

## Proverbs 4:7 (id: 10020004007)

7Waestok ya, hem i nambawan samting blong yu yu kasem. I nomata wanem samting we yu yu gat, be yu mas kasem fasin ya blong luksave mining blong ol samting we i had.

## Proverbs 4:8 (id: 10020004008)

8Sipos yu laekem waestok ya, bambae hem i save leftemap yu, we nem blong yu bambae i go antap. Sipos yu holemtaet hem, bambae hem i save mekem ol man oli ona gud long yu.

## Proverbs 4:9 (id: 10020004009)

9Nao hem bambae i kam olsem ol gudfala flaoa ya, we ol man oli hangem long nek blong yu blong ona long yu. Bambae hem i kam olsem wan gudfala samting blong flasem hed blong yu, olsem hat blong king we i saen we i saen.”

## Proverbs 4:10 (id: 10020004010)

10Pikinini blong mi. Sipos yu lesin gud long mi, mo yu tinghevi long ol samting ya we mi stap talem long yu, bambae yu gat longfala laef.

## Proverbs 4:11 (id: 10020004011)

11Mi mi stap tijim yu long ol waestok, mo long ol stret fasin we yu mas folem long laef blong yu,

## Proverbs 4:12 (id: 10020004012)

12mo sipos yu stap folem waestok ya, bambae i no gat wan samting i save blokem yu, mo sipos yu resis, bambae yu no save foldaon.

## Proverbs 4:13 (id: 10020004013)

13Oltaem, yu mas tingbaot ol samting ya we yu lanem finis. Olgeta oli stamba blong laef blong yu, mo yu mas lukaot gud long olgeta.

## Proverbs 4:14 (id: 10020004014)

14Bambae yu no mas folem rod ya, we ol man nogud oli stap folem, mo yu no mas tekem fasin blong olgeta.

## Proverbs 4:16 (id: 10020004016)

## Proverbs 4:17 (id: 10020004017)

17Fasin nogud mo fasin blong faet, hemia i olsem kakae mo wota blong olgeta.

## Proverbs 4:19 (id: 10020004019)

19Be rod blong ol man nogud i tudak olgeta, i olsem naet. Oltaem olgeta oli stap foldaon, be oli no save luksave ol samting we oli stambol long hem.

## Proverbs 4:20 (id: 10020004020)

20Pikinini blong mi. Yu mas lesin gud long ol samting ya we mi stap talem long yu. Yu mas harem ol tok ya blong mi.
