## 2 Corinthians 1:1 (id: 10047001001)

1Mi Pol, mi aposol blong Jisas Kraes\*, we God i putumap mi. Mi wetem Timoti, brata blong yumi, mitufala i talem halo long yufala long jos blong God long Korin, mo long olgeta man blong God long olgeta ples long Akaea\*.

## 2 Corinthians 1:2 (id: 10047001002)

2Prea blong mitufala, we God, Papa blong yumi, wetem Jisas Kraes, Masta\* blong yumi, tufala bambae i stap givhan long yufala long gladhat blong tufala, tufala i putum pis i stap wetem yufala.

## 2 Corinthians 1:3 (id: 10047001003)

3I gud yumi stap talem tangkiu long God ya we i God mo Papa blong Jisas Kraes, Masta blong yumi. Hem i Papa blong yumi we i gat sore, hem i God we i stamba blong fasin blong givhan.

## 2 Corinthians 1:4 (id: 10047001004)

## 2 Corinthians 1:5 (id: 10047001005)

5Bifo, Kraes i harem nogud tumas long bodi blong hem, mo ol fasin ya we i kasem hem, i kasem mifala tu. Mo long sem fasin, from we mifala i joen long Kraes, God i stap mekem mifala i harem gud, mo fasin ya i go moa, i kasem ol narafala man.

## 2 Corinthians 1:6 (id: 10047001006)

Nao taem we yufala i stap harem nogud olsem mifala, bambae tingting blong yufala i longfala, i no save foldaon.
